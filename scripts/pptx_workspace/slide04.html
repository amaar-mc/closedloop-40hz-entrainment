Methods

Dataset

  • OpenNeuro ds005048 (Lahijanian et al., 2024)
  • 35 elderly subjects: Alzheimer’s disease (n = 17), MCI (n = 6), healthy controls (n = 10), unspecified (n = 2)
  • 7 frontal EEG channels (Fp1, Fp2, F7, F3, Fz, F4, F8), sampled at 250 Hz
  • Protocol: 40 Hz auditory stimulation (40 s ON / 20 s OFF cycles)

Preprocessing

  • Bandpass filter: 0.5–80 Hz (4th-order Butterworth, zero-phase)
  • Notch filter: 50 Hz (Q = 30) for power-line removal
  • Artifact rejection: ±100 µV threshold (applied before CAR)
  • Common average reference (after artifact rejection)
  • Windowing: 2-second windows (500 samples), 1-second hop

Subject-Level Data Splits (No Within-Subject Leakage)

  • Training: 24 subjects (11,736 windows)
  • Validation: 5 subjects (2,725 windows)
  • Test: 6 subjects (2,822 windows) — never seen during training or hyperparameter selection

Phase-Amplitude Coupling (PAC) Computation

  • Modulation Index (Tort et al., 2010): measures coordination between theta phase (4–8 Hz) and gamma amplitude (38–42 Hz)
  • KL divergence from uniform distribution over 18 phase bins (20° each)
  • Computed at epoch level (20–40 s); assigned to all constituent 2-second windows within each epoch
  • Range: 6e-6 to 7e-4 (dimensionless MI units); mean approx. 4.4e-5
