## Statement (ready to adapt into the author's own words)

> I used AI tools in two supporting roles, both disclosed.
>
> **Software.** I used an AI coding assistant while writing and debugging the analysis code — data loading, PAC computation, model training, and the stress-test harness — as I would use documentation or a teaching assistant. >
> **Writing.** I used an AI writing assistant to help organize and draft this abstract from my own results and notes. The content — the method, the numbers, and the target-definition finding — is mine. I verified every number against my own results files.
>
> No AI system generated the data, the experiments, or the conclusions.
