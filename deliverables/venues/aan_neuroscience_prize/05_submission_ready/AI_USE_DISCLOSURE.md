# AI-Use Disclosure — AAN / Bhuwan Garg High School Neuroscience Research Prize

**Read this before submitting.** AAN requires that the application be "the original research and written work of the applicant." There is no explicit AI ban, but that originality clause is close enough that AI-assisted drafting must be handled carefully. See the residual-risk note in `MANUSCRIPT_SELF_CHECK.md`.

## Statement (ready to adapt into the applicant's own words)

> During this project I used AI tools in two supporting roles, both of which I am disclosing.
>
> First, in building the software: I used an AI coding assistant while writing and debugging the analysis pipeline (data loading, PAC computation, model training, and the controller-replay harness), in the way I would use documentation, a textbook, or a teaching assistant's office hours. I made every design decision, chose the models and features, ran the experiments, and checked the results myself.
>
> Second, in preparing this written submission: I used an AI writing assistant to help organize and draft prose from my own results, notes, and figures. The scientific content — the question, the dataset choice, the methods, the numbers, the target-definition stress test, and the interpretation of the mixed controller result — is mine. I verified every reported number against my own results files and confirmed every citation against the original paper.
>
> No AI system generated the data, the experiments, or the scientific conclusions of this work.

## Important caveat for the applicant

Because AAN asks for the applicant's own original written work, the safest path is **one of these two, done before submitting**:

1. **Rewrite the report in your own words.** Use this drafted text as a reference and a source of your verified numbers, then write the submission yourself. This fully satisfies the originality clause and is the recommended option for AAN specifically.
2. **Confirm in writing that AI-assisted drafting with disclosure is acceptable.** Email science@aan.com, describe the two AI roles above, and get an answer before you submit.

Keep your drafts, notebooks, and result files. They are evidence of authorship if the writing is ever questioned — which matters because AI-text detectors are unreliable and can false-flag genuine work.
