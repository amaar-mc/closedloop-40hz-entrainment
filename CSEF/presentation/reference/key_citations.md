## Core Four

### Iaccarino et al. (2016)

- Why it matters: the landmark MIT paper that showed 40 Hz stimulation reduced amyloid burden in mice
- Your hook: "This is the paper that made 40 Hz therapy scientifically serious."

### Murdock et al. (2024)

- Why it matters: clarified the glymphatic clearance mechanism involving aquaporin-4 pathways
- Your hook: "This paper helped explain how 40 Hz can drive waste clearance, not just correlate with it."

### Lahijanian et al. (2024)

- Why it matters: the human auditory 40 Hz dataset you used
- Your hook: "This is my data source: 35 elderly subjects receiving auditory 40 Hz stimulation."

### Tort et al. (2010)

- Why it matters: standard Modulation Index method for computing PAC
- Your hook: "This is why my biomarker is grounded in an established method."
