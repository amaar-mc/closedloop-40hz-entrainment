## Disposition Legend

- `keep` - retain because the claim already matches repository evidence.
- `rewrite` - keep the topic, but adjust wording, date, scope, or framing.
- `drop` - remove because the claim is unsupported, stale, or misleading.
- `gap note` - replace weak day-level detail with a shorter supported span note.
