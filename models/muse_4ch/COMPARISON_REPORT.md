## Interpretation

The performance delta between 7-channel and 4-channel models quantifies the cost of adapting from research-grade frontal coverage to consumer hardware (Muse 2) electrode positions.

**Important caveats:**

- R2 values are NOT directly comparable across channel configurations because the PAC labels differ (mean MI across different channel sets).
- Each model should be evaluated against its own persistence/Ridge baselines.
- At deployment, the domain gap (dry vs gel electrodes, reference mismatch) will further degrade performance beyond what this proxy comparison shows.
