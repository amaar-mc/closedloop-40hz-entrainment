**07-discussion.md — Discussion section with 4 subsections:**

7.1 Interpretation of the Prediction Horizon Inflection Point:
- The ~3s inflection is the central finding: below this, simpler models suffice; above it, temporal modeling is essential
- Explain WHY persistence fails at longer horizons: PAC dynamics are non-stationary, and the autocorrelation structure decays after ~3s
- The TCN's causal dilated convolutions (dilations [1,2,4,8]) capture multi-scale temporal patterns that persistence and Ridge cannot
- Clinical significance: 5-10s lead time is what a real-time system needs for stimulus preparation

7.2 Why Proactive Outperforms Reactive Control:
- Reactive control has inherent latency: it detects decline AFTER it happens
- Proactive (TCN) pre-positions: it predicts decline and acts before it occurs
- The 0.8s vs 0.2s lead time difference translates to the 7.6% alignment improvement
- Trade-off: TCN sacrifices High-PAC Rest Rate (61.6% vs 77.3%) for dramatically better Low-PAC Stim Rate (82.6% vs 51.7%)
- This trade-off is clinically favorable: the cost of unnecessary stimulation is low (it's auditory), while the cost of missing therapeutic windows is high

7.3 Comparison to Prior Closed-Loop Neuromodulation Work:
- Position relative to Portiloop (PLOS ONE 2022): similar DL approach but for sleep spindles, not gamma entrainment
- Position relative to Scalable Framework (PMC 2023): uses classification vs our regression+forecasting
- Position relative to DBS work (Rosin 2011, Herron 2017): invasive vs our non-invasive approach
- What makes this work novel: (1) PAC-specific forecasting, (2) horizon-dependent characterization, (3) all-subject benefit with real EEG validation

7.4 Limitations:
- Brief and forward-looking per user decision
- Key limitations to acknowledge explicitly:
  1. Validation is offline counterfactual replay — not live closed-loop with real brain response to stimulation
  2. EEGNet is not in the validation loop — TCN receives ground-truth PAC, not estimated PAC
  3. Dataset is from one clinical site (Tehran memory clinic) — generalizability to other populations unknown
  4. 7 frontal channels may miss relevant parietal/temporal sources
  5. The R-squared=0.287 static ceiling may be partly due to PAC labeling granularity (epoch-level labels for 2s windows)
- For each limitation, briefly note how future work could address it (linking to Future Directions)
