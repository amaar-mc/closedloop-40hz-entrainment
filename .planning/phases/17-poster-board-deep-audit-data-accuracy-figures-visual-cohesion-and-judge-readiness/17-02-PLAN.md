**1. Hypothesis and Scientific Rigor:**
- Is the hypothesis clearly stated and falsifiable?
- Is the experimental design described with sufficient detail for a judge to understand the methodology?
- Are controls adequate and clearly identified? (Fixed Schedule, Reactive, Oracle, persistence baseline, shuffle-label test, multi-seed robustness, threshold sensitivity)
- Is the sample size (N=35) prominently stated?

**2. Results Framing:**
- Are results presented honestly without overclaiming?
- Is the "Summary of Key Results" box clear and compelling?
- Are effect sizes reported with confidence intervals (not just p-values)?
- Is the 35/35 subjects claim supported with appropriate statistical test (binomial)?
- Do the four numbered results tell a coherent story?

**3. Limitations and Honesty:**
- Is the offline replay limitation clearly disclosed?
- Is the simulated fatigue model limitation acknowledged?
- Are there any claims that a judge might challenge as overclaiming?
- **Specifically assess:** Does the poster imply the TCN controller validation used the 5,154-param model when it actually used the 31,043-param model? How visible is this inconsistency?

**4. Judge Risk Analysis:**
Document each identified risk from the research with a severity rating (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) and a recommended verbal response for Amaar to use during judging:

- **Risk 1 (HIGH): TCN param count inconsistency** — Pipeline says 5,154 but validation used 31,043. Recommended response: explain the architecture search found the efficient model, but validation was completed before re-running with the reduced model.
- **Risk 2 (MEDIUM): Stale R2=0.25 in Conclusion 1** — Figure 6 shows 0.577-0.669. Recommended response: acknowledge the conclusion text refers to the original 73-feature model while Figure 6 shows the improved PAC+Stim model.
- **Risk 3 (LOW): Offline replay acknowledged** — Already disclosed in limitations.
- **Risk 4 (LOW): "Dementia patients" claim** — Verified correct per Lahijanian 2024.
- **Risk 5 (MEDIUM): Fatigue results use heuristic controller, not TCN** — Not explicitly disclosed. Recommended response.
- **Risk 6 (LOW-MEDIUM): Exponential Decay g=2.01 vs 2.313** — Factual error. Recommended response.

**5. References:**
- Check all 8 references for completeness (authors, journal, volume, pages, year)
- Flag Reference 1's "76323" formatting issue (should be Nature volume/issue, not this code)
- Verify Reference 7 (Chan 2025) year — is this published or forthcoming?
- Are all in-text citations properly linked to the reference list?

**6. Future Directions and Clinical Use:**
- Are these sections realistic and scientifically grounded?
- Does the 3-phase clinical roadmap (observational, feasibility, comparative) reflect real clinical trial design?
- Is the productization narrative (caregiver app, Muse 2 headband) honest about current status?
