## Decisions Made

- Used `norm_first=True` (pre-LayerNorm) in TransformerEncoderLayer for stable gradients; this triggers a PyTorch UserWarning about nested tensors (benign — just a performance optimization that doesn't apply with norm_first).
- Computed per-subject causal trailing mean for auxiliary smooth targets rather than global convolution. The NPZ stores samples in subject-contiguous blocks, but a global convolve would bleed the last samples of subject N into the first samples of subject N+1. Per-subject grouping via the subjects array prevents this contamination.
- Auxiliary smooth head used only during training loss; never in evaluation. This preserves apples-to-apples R2 comparison with the baseline MultiscaleCausalTCN.
- Reported negative delta_r2 on 7ch honestly. The improved model has ~2.3x more parameters than baseline (70K vs 31K) and the additional attention capacity appears to overfit the 7ch 11K-sample training set.
