 Background
40 Hz gamma entrainment reduces amyloid load in Alzheimer's
mouse models by activating microglia (Iaccarino 2016); multi-
sensory stimulation reduces tau pathology and improves
cognition (Martorell 2019); auditory entrainment enhances
default mode network connectivity in human dementia patients
(Lahijanian 2024)
