## Hard Gates

- Do not claim an affiliation, mentor, or lab unless the person or institution genuinely agrees and is specific to this work.
- Do not submit STS or JSHS generated prose, or close paraphrases of it. Use the blueprints only to understand structure; write those applications yourself and disclose tool assistance where required.
- Do not submit BMES General Abstract as a high-school sole presenter unless BMES confirms eligibility.
- Do not submit SfN late-breaking unless there is genuinely new post-regular-deadline analysis and the SfN membership/account requirements are satisfied.
- Do not submit AAN without parent/guardian, teacher, and mentor e-signatures.
- Do not submit JSHS until the current Northern California packet opens and you have written the abstract and paper yourself.
- Do not email SPMB until the draft is converted into the required PDF/template format.
- Do not treat Sigma Xi as ready until the website, technical slideshow, and video are actually built.
- Do not start Synopsys without the required adult sponsor, ISEF/SCVSEFA forms, and research plan.
- Do not claim clinical efficacy, prospective deployment, or patient benefit.
- Do not force an identity, PI, or community-impact narrative for AISES or SACNAS.
