# MIT URTC Submission Requirements

**Verified:** 2026-05-31  
**Target cycle:** Fall 2026  
**Current limitation:** the official site still publishes 2025 dates, links, and guidelines.

## What Is Confirmed From The Latest Official Material

MIT URTC is not an abstract-only application for a paper presentation. The official submission page
asks for a research manuscript submitted through CMT. The latest published guideline states:

- English-language research paper.
- Maximum length: 5 single-spaced pages. The guideline permits figures, illustrations, and graphs.
- Minimum font size: 10 points.
- Include an abstract of no more than 500 words within the paper.
- Submit electronically through the conference CMT portal.
- At least one author of an accepted paper must register and present at the conference.
- The current submission page says reviewers evaluate originality, quality of content, and
  adherence to conference themes.
- Reviews are performed by faculty members, graduate students, and industry professionals.
- Accepted and presented papers may be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore.

## Eligibility Confirmation Needed

The official submission page gives separate high-school presentation rules:

- Poster presentation: open to undergraduate and high-school students.
- Paper presentation: high-school students must either be affiliated with a collegiate research
  program or have worked substantially with university students.

The official FAQ gives a broader, practical explanation of the high-school paper route. Qualifying
affiliation may include an official faculty-mentored research program, informal mentorship by
university faculty, or substantial collaboration with an undergraduate or graduate student. AP,
IB, and other college-level high-school programs do not qualify by themselves. A high-school
student without a qualifying university connection may still be eligible for a poster or lightning
talk.

The homepage also states that minors must be accompanied by an adult at all times during the
conference.

The 2025 guideline PDF and public submission page differ on the maximum number of presenters, so
that detail must be rechecked in the 2026 portal before submission.

The currently published formatting artifacts also differ on first-page structure. The 2025
guideline PDF says that only the paper title should appear on the title page. The Word template
linked from MIT's submission page begins with the paper title, author block, abstract, and keywords
before the two-column paper body. The current-baseline proof follows the linked Word template
structure. Resolve this discrepancy against the confirmed Fall 2026 instructions before
submission.
