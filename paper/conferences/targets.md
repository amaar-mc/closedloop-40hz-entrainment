# Conference Targets

## How to choose

This work sits at the intersection of:
- Neural signal processing / BCI
- Closed-loop neurostimulation
- Clinical ML / Alzheimer's disease
- Temporal deep learning (TCN, causal prediction)

Appropriate venues span ML conferences (workshop tracks), biomedical engineering, and clinical neuroscience.

---

## Tier 1 — Primary targets

| Conference | Full name | Track | Typical deadline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IEEE EMBC** | Engineering in Medicine and Biology | Main / BCI track | Jan-Feb | Most direct fit. Peer-reviewed, 4-page format |
| **NeurIPS Workshop** | Neural Information Processing Systems | BCI / neuroscience workshop | Aug-Sep | High visibility; workshop = lower bar than main |
| **ICLR Workshop** | International Conference on Learning Representations | NeuroAI / BCI workshop | Jan-Feb | Strong ML credibility |

## Tier 2 — Strong alternatives

| Conference | Full name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PRNI** | Pattern Recognition in NeuroImaging | Small, focused, high acceptance |
| **AIME** | Artificial Intelligence in Medicine | Clinical AI focus |
| **Brain Stimulation (journal)** | Elsevier | Journal not conference — longer timeline but higher impact |
| **IEEE TNSRE** | Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering | Journal; peer-reviewed |

## Formatting notes by venue

### IEEE EMBC
- 4 pages + 1 page references (strict)
- IEEE two-column format
- LaTeX template: `IEEEtran.cls`
- Double-blind review

### NeurIPS Workshop
- 4-8 pages (workshop-dependent)
- NeurIPS style
- Often single-blind

### ICLR Workshop
- 4-6 pages
- ICLR style (`iclr2025.sty`)
- Often non-archival (can still submit to main venues after)
