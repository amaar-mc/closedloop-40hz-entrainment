### INTRODUCTION (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

Alzheimer's disease affects over 55 million people worldwide with no cure. Recent research shows 40 Hz auditory stimulation synchronizes brain gamma oscillations, reducing amyloid-beta plaques and tau tangles in animal models (Iaccarino 2016) and showing cognitive benefits in human trials (Chan 2025).

Current protocols deliver stimulation on a fixed schedule — 40s ON / 20s OFF, cycling for one hour regardless of how the patient's brain responds. This ignores that some patients maintain strong theta-gamma coupling throughout a session while others lose it within seconds. Prolonged stimulation also causes neural habituation, where the brain "tunes out" the stimulus. The result: wasted stimulation during strong coupling and missed therapeutic windows during fading coupling.

**Can we predict when a patient's brain will lose entrainment and stimulate proactively, before decline occurs?**

### BACKGROUND (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

**[FIGURE 2: PAC Concept Diagram — ~8" x 4"]**

*(see Figure Generation Prompts below)*

**Phase-Amplitude Coupling (PAC)** is the standard biomarker for entrainment quality. It measures how strongly gamma-band amplitude (38-42 Hz) locks to the phase of theta oscillations (4-8 Hz). High PAC means the therapy is driving neural synchrony. Low PAC means entrainment has been lost. No existing system predicts *future* PAC — fixed schedules cannot adapt at all, and reactive thresholds only respond *after* coupling has already declined.

---

### HYPOTHESIS (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

A causal Temporal Convolutional Network trained on PAC trajectory features can forecast coupling dynamics 5-10 seconds ahead — a horizon where simpler baselines collapse — enabling a closed-loop controller that delivers stimulation proactively rather than reactively.

**Goals:**
1. Predict brain entrainment state 5-10 seconds ahead
2. Build a personalized closed-loop stimulation controller
3. Validate on real patient EEG from 35 subjects (17 AD, 6 MCI, 10 controls)

### METHODS (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

**Dataset**
- OpenNeuro ds005048 (Lahijanian 2024)
- 35 elderly subjects (17 AD, 6 MCI, 10 controls)
- 7 frontal EEG channels, 250 Hz
- Protocol: 40 Hz auditory stim (40s ON / 20s OFF)

**Preprocessing**
- Bandpass 0.5-80 Hz, notch 50 Hz
- Artifact rejection +-100 uV, CAR
- 2s windows (500 samples)

**Model: MultiscaleCausalTCN**
- 5,154 parameters (h=32)
- 4 causal conv blocks, dilations [1,2,4,8]
- 12 features: 7 PAC trajectory + 5 stim context
- Huber loss, AdamW, early stopping (patience=20)

---

### DATA SPLITS & VALIDATION (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

**Subject-level splits (no leakage)**
- Train: 24 subjects (11,736 windows)
- Validation: 5 subjects (2,725 windows)
- Test: 6 subjects (2,822 windows)

**Validation protocol**
- Offline counterfactual replay on all 35 subjects
- Ground-truth PAC labels as TCN input
- 6 controller variants including oracle upper bound

**Statistics**
- Wilcoxon signed-rank (paired, non-parametric)
- Hedges' g + 95% BCa bootstrap CIs
- 5-seed reproducibility: R-squared = 0.606 +- 0.032
- Shuffle-label R-squared = -0.332 (signal is real)

---

### CONCLUSIONS (header: Amaranth Bold 20pt white on teal)

1. **Feature selection > architecture:** 12 PAC features outperform 73 spectral features five-fold (R-squared -0.025 to 0.606)
2. TCN maintains R-squared = 0.37-0.67 at 3-10s where all baselines collapse
3. **72.1% alignment** vs 64.5% reactive (p < 0.001), reaching **91% of oracle**
4. **All 35 subjects benefited** (p < 0.001), including 6 held-out test subjects
5. Robust across z-score thresholds 0.2-1.0 and four fatigue models

**Limitation:** Offline replay on recorded EEG, not live closed-loop streaming.
