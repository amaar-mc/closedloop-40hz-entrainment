### Prohibited Items (mostly the same, some differences)

**Same at both:**
- Living organisms, preserved animals, hazardous materials, open flames, sharp items, glass, chemicals/liquids, food, weapons, photos of surgical procedures, photos of human subjects without consent

**Synopsys-specific prohibitions NOT explicitly listed at CSEF:**

- Drones/flight apparatus (unless power removed)
- 3D printers (unless power removed)
- Distracting displays (sounds, lights, odors)
- Distribution items (disks, CDs, flash drives, brochures, business cards)
- Previous fair awards or medals on display
- Patent status references

**CSEF-specific notes:**
- Using aisle space (even temporarily during interviews) is **cause for disqualification**
- Displays augmented after admission to exceed size limits → **disqualified until fixed**
- Floor displays allowed but cannot use table space

## 2. Project Presentation (13-Page PDF) — NEW FOR YOU

**This does not exist at Synopsys.** At CSEF, you must submit a 13-page max PDF that is **reviewed and approved before the fair**. You already submitted yours.

Key rules:
- Single PDF, max 13 pages
- Must follow one of three templates: Experimental, Engineering, or Analysis
- Five sections in fixed order, each starting on its own page
- Must include: Research Question, Project Origin, Scope of Work (with two subsections), References
- Figures/graphs allowed within page limit
- 14pt minimum font size
- Must be approved — check status at sciencefair.space
- **Print it and bring it to your display**

## 3. Judging

This is the biggest process difference.

### Synopsys Judging
- **40-point numerical rubric** across 4 dimensions (10 pts each):
  1. Scientific Thought / Engineering Goals
  2. Creativity
  3. Independent Work / Skill
  4. Thoroughness / Clarity
- At least 2 category judges interview each project (~15 min each)
- Separate Grand Prize judges for grades 9-12
- Category + Sponsored award judges visit

### CSEF Judging
- **No numerical scoring** — winners chosen by "overwhelming agreement of the entire panel"
- Judges **pre-read your abstract AND 13-page presentation** before interviews
- Multiple interviews throughout the day (12:45–4:30 PM)
- Must complete at least **3 Grand Award Judge interviews by 3:00 PM**
- Both Grand Award and Special Award judges conduct interviews
- Six evaluation dimensions (not scored numerically):
  1. Originality / Innovation
  2. Scientific Method / Experimental Design
  3. Research Plan
  4. Data Analysis and Conclusions
  5. Display and Communication
  6. Compliance

### What This Means for You
- **Judges arrive already informed.** Don't waste time on basics they've read. Focus your 1-2 min summary on what's surprising or impressive.
- **Expect deeper questions.** They've had time to formulate specific questions from your PDF.
- **More interviews over a longer period.** Pace yourself. Stay energized.
- **No rubric = no gaming the rubric.** Overall impression and depth of understanding matter more than hitting checkbox criteria.
- **Consensus model favors memorable projects.** One judge being impressed isn't enough — the whole panel must agree.

## 4. Forms and Paperwork

### Same at Both
- Form 1 (Adult Sponsor Checklist)
- Form 1A (Student Checklist)
- Form 1B (Approval Form)
- Form 1C (if research at institution)
- Form 4 (human participants)
- Form 5A/5B (vertebrate animals)
- Form 7 (continuation projects)

### Synopsys-Specific Requirements
- **Ethics Statement** (signed, each team member)
- **Assistance Disclosure Form** (signed)
- **Photo/Video/Media Release Form** (each team member)
- **Student Permission and Hold Harmless Agreement**
- **Project notebook upload** (4-page PDF excerpt for abstract submission)

### CSEF-Specific Requirements
- **13-page Project Presentation PDF** (must be pre-approved)
- ISEF forms required for Senior Division students wanting ISEF consideration
- CSEF Certification Form sufficient for Junior Division

### Key Difference
Synopsys requires more standalone administrative forms (ethics, assistance disclosure, media release). CSEF folds most of this into the ISEF form set and the 13-page presentation.

## 6. AI / Generative AI Rules

Both follow ISEF rules. **Identical policies:**

**OK without citation:** Grammar/syntax fixes, summarizing articles as starting point, fixing bibliography formatting.

**OK with citation + prompt log:** Idea generation, initial code writing, flowcharts/graphics (marked as AI-generated), statistical test selection, guidance after you've written the initial draft.

**Never OK:** AI writing your research plan/abstract/paper/poster, AI producing your conclusions, AI providing citations, AI writing your bibliography, reproducing AI summaries without reading sources.

**Disclosure:** All AI use must be documented. At Synopsys this is the Assistance Disclosure Form. At CSEF it's part of the ISEF form set.
