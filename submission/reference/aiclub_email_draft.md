The project is called "Personalized Deep Learning Model for Closed-Loop 40 Hz Entrainment to Optimize Theta-Gamma Coupling in Alzheimer's Disease." The short version: I built a deep learning system that predicts when an Alzheimer's patient's brain will lose response to 40 Hz auditory therapy, 5 seconds before it happens, and uses that prediction to adapt stimulation timing in real time. I trained a causal Temporal Convolutional Network on EEG recordings from 35 elderly subjects, integrated it into a closed-loop controller, and validated it against five alternative control strategies. The predictive controller achieved 72.1% alignment with therapeutic need versus 64.5% for reactive control (p < 0.001), and every single subject showed improvement. The full paper is about 5,500 words with 7 figures, 5 tables, 23 references, and 8 detailed limitations. I've attached the PDF along with my poster board from Synopsys Championship.
