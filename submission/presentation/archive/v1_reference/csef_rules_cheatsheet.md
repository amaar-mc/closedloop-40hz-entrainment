# CSEF 2026 Rules Cheatsheet

Print this. Know it.

---

## Schedule
| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| **Sat April 11** | Setup day. Mount poster, arrange table, test laptop. |
| **Sun April 12, ~12:45 PM** | Judging begins |
| **~3:00 PM** | Must have completed at least 3 Grand Award judge interviews |
| **~4:30 PM** | Judging ends |
| **After judging** | Panel caucus (judges only), then awards |

*Note: The 2019 handbook schedule differs (8:30 AM start). Use the 2026 times from CSEF website/confirmation email.*

## Judging Format
| | Details |
|---|---|
| Format | One judge at a time, one-on-one |
| Interviews per student | At least 5 (guideline) |
| Interview length | 5-15 min each (avg ~8-10 min for large panels) |
| Judging style | NO numerical rubric. Winners by consensus of full panel. |
| Judges pre-read | Your 13-page presentation AND abstract before interviews |
| Two rounds | First Round + Break (judges identify finalists) + Final Round |

## Five Judging Criteria (not scored, but evaluated)
1. **Originality** -- new approach, creative use of resources
2. **Comprehension** -- depth of understanding, ability to explain
3. **Organization & Completeness** -- well-defined goal, thorough execution
4. **Effort & Motivation** -- time invested, genuine interest
5. **Clarity** -- clear communication, good visual aids

## What Gets High Marks (from handbook p.20)
- Genuine scientific breakthroughs
- Discovering knowledge not readily available
- Correctly interpreting data
- Repetitions to verify results
- Ability to clearly explain the project and results
- Predicting/reducing experimental results with analytical techniques

## What Gets Low Marks
- Ignoring readily available information
- Improperly using jargon / not understanding terminology
- Not knowing how equipment works
- Presenting results not from your own experimentation

## Prohibited at Display
- QR codes on display board
- Distributing materials to judges (cards, handouts, USB drives)
- Using aisle space (even temporarily) -- **disqualification risk**
- Previous fair awards/medals on display
- AC power (none provided)
- School name on board

## What Must Be at Display
- Poster board (printed, 48x64 in max)
- Printed 13-page project presentation
- Lab notebook (recommended, not mandatory)
- You, standing at your display

## Category: Medicine & Physiology
- Judges are medical/physiology domain experts
- They are NOT required to evaluate computational innovations
- Focus: disease understanding, therapeutic contribution, clinical potential
- Your ML pipeline is a TOOL, not the focus they're required to evaluate

## Project of the Year (if First Place)
- Panel representative presents 1-minute synopsis (strictly enforced)
- Can be up to 3 minutes in extended format
- POTY judges are from ALL categories (not just medicine)
- Criteria: original thought, scientific impact, societal impact, worldly application
- 90-minute session, must be done by 3:00 PM

## ISEF Advancement
- ~3 Senior Division projects advance from CSEF to ISEF
- Selected from First Place category winners
- ISEF 2026: May 9-15, Phoenix, AZ

## Key Differences from Synopsys
| | Synopsys | CSEF |
|---|---|---|
| Rubric | 40-point numerical | No rubric, consensus |
| Judges pre-read | No | Yes (13-page + abstract) |
| Interview style | ~15 min, 2+ judges | 5-15 min each, 5+ judges |
| Display depth | 15 in | 30 in (2x deeper) |
| AC power | Available | Not available |
