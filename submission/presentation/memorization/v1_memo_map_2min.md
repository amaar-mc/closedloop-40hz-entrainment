# Memorization Map: 2-Minute Overview

Do not memorize sentences. Memorize seven beats.

---

## The Seven Beats

1. Scale
2. Why 40 Hz matters
3. Fixed schedule problem
4. What I built
5. How I got there
6. Results
7. Limitation and next step

## One-Line Anchor For Each Beat

1. Scale: "55 million people and limited treatment options."
2. Why 40 Hz matters: "40 Hz auditory stimulation can improve theta-gamma synchronization and support clearance pathways."
3. Fixed schedule problem: "40 seconds on, 20 seconds off ignores real patient variability."
4. What I built: "A system that measures PAC and predicts 5 seconds ahead."
5. How I got there: "Eight snapshot models hit R^2 = 0.287, so I switched to forecasting."
6. Results: "72.1 vs 64.5, 82.6 vs 51.7, 35 out of 35."
7. Limitation and next step: "Offline replay now, live crossover study next."

## Transition Chain

- "That is why the disease burden matters."
- "But promising biology is not enough if delivery is blind."
- "That is the problem I focused on."
- "So I built a system that adapts."
- "The key insight came from the static ceiling."
- "That led to the main result."
- "The honest limitation is..."

## What To Picture While Speaking

- Beat 1: big picture, public health problem
- Beat 2: brain therapy concept
- Beat 3: fixed timer failing real patients
- Beat 4: your system listening and predicting
- Beat 5: the pivot in your thinking
- Beat 6: the result table
- Beat 7: a future live study

## 10-Minute Practice Method

1. Say only the seven beat names twice.
2. Expand each beat into two sentences.
3. Do one full timed run.
4. Repeat only the transitions.
5. Do one more full run without looking.
